OmniVoice Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction

Volvevent ("we", "us", or "our") operates the OmniVoice mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

2. Data Processing and AI Voice Cloning

OmniVoice is built with a strict "Zero Cloud" architecture. All voice cloning, audio processing, and AI synthesis happen strictly on your local device hardware. We do not upload, record, store, or transmit your voice data, audio files, or transcripts to any external servers.

3. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

While the core App is fully offline, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (if you have not purchased the Ad-Free version). AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as your IP address and mobile advertising ID, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

For more information on how Google uses this data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms site:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Device Permissions

The App requires access to your device's microphone and local storage exclusively for the purpose of generating the AI models locally. This data never leaves your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at our Developer email address.
